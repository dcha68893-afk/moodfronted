Duka Smart

Duka Smart is the business tool built into Necpra. Log in with your normal Necpra account, open Duka Smart and manage your shop from your phone. There is no separate sign-up.

Expenses, suppliers and credit

Keep track of what you spend, who supplies you, debts and customer credit in one place.

Keep working when the network is weak and let your records sync when you are back online.
