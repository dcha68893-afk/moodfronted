NECPRA Money

Organize everyday money together.

NECPRA Money brings group collections and payment requests into the same platform as NECPRA's social, marketplace and digital services.

Money Circles

Create a circle for a family contribution, trip, event, project, emergency fund or other shared goal. Members can see the purpose and contribution activity, while M-Pesa contributions are only recorded as paid after the payment provider confirms the transaction.

Pay for Someone

Create a structured request for a person or service you intend to pay. Requests are tied to an authenticated NECPRA account, have an expiry window and use abuse controls. A request itself is not proof that money is owed.

Safety and privacy

NECPRA should never be used as proof that a stranger is trustworthy. Verify the person and purpose independently before paying. Never share an M-Pesa PIN, password or one-time code. Private requests, balances and transaction records belong inside authenticated areas of the app rather than public search pages.

Payment verification

NECPRA does not treat a user's claim as payment confirmation. M-Pesa payment status is confirmed through the configured payment callback and verification flow. Future person-to-person disbursement features require the appropriate Safaricom production capability.
