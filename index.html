💡 Quick Tips

1
Turn on AI Translation in a chat's settings before joining a global room — messages arrive already translated, no copy-pasting.
2
Add 2–3 interests to your profile first. Rooms and people are suggested from them, so a filled-out profile means better matches from minute one.
3
On video calls, join with your mic muted and camera off — you can turn both on once you're in, and it avoids a noisy entrance.
4
Pin your most active chats from the chat list menu so they stay at the top instead of getting buried by new conversations.
