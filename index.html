💡 Quick Tips

1

2

Pin your most active chats from the chat list menu so they stay at the top instead of getting buried by new conversations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Nexopa free to use?

Yes — messaging, calls, groups and communities are free. Some marketplace and premium tools may have their own pricing.

Are my messages private?

Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted, so only you and the people in the conversation can read them.

Can I use Nexopa on multiple devices?

Yes — sign in on any device and your chats, calls and groups stay in sync.

Does Nexopa work offline?

Nexopa is a Progressive Web App with offline support — recent chats stay available and messages send once you're back online.
